Disable Steam Workshop Subscription Management

Manage Steam Workshop Subscriptions ››

You can click Stop Managing Steam Workshop Content on the ribbon's Customise tab to stop the Tool's management of your Workshop Subscriptions or use Preferences in Advanced Settings to disable the management of your Workshop Subscriptions.


  1. Use the Customise tab on the Ribbon to stop managing Workshop Content

    or disable "Use NIT to manage Steam's Workshop Content" on the Preferences page in Advanced Settings and click Save.
  2. You are asked what you want do with Tool's version of each Workshop Subscription.
  3. You can specify whether you want to take the same action for all managed Workshop Mods.
  4. The Web Link containing the location of the Workshop Item Folder is cleared for any Mods you retain.


Steam Workshop Subscription information is retained so that Identifier to Mod Name mapping preferences are available in case you enable management again.
