Community Expansion Pack (CEP)

Work with Mods ›› Surazal's Mod notes ››

The latest version of CEP v2.x contains all the files required and includes CEP v1.x Complete and CEP v2.2.


If you have previously downloaded and installed CEP v1.x Complete and CEP v2.2, you should leave them unchanged to ensure that your Mod dependencies are retained.

I recommend renaming CEP v2.65 to CEP v2.x to comply with the new CEP Project Page Title.
