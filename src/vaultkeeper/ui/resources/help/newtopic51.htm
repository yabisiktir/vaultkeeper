Send diagnostic information to Surazal

Neverwinter Nights Installer Tool ›› Frequently asked questions (FAQ) ››

Follow this procedure to send diagnostic information to Surazal when you are having non-fatal issues with the Installer Tool and want to report the problem.


  1. Click Send Diagnostic Information from the Help menu after experiencing the issue you wish to report.
  2. An email message is generated using your default mail application.
  3. Press Ctrl+V to paste the diagnostic information file into the email and click Send.


I will investigate the problem and respond to your email with the results of my diagnosis.
