Specify your Preferences

Customise the Installer Tool ››

Use the Preferences page to adjust the Installer Tool's appearance, behaviour, how it deals with Mods, files and folders to suite your own tastes.


Click the Check Box, Double-Click or press the Space Bar to enable or disable Preferences. You can also Right-Click specific Preferences if you prefer to use a menu.


Select a Preference to display details in the information panel.


Preferences you change during your current Settings session are displayed in italics as shown in this illustration.
