Perform Find Operations

The Installer Tool's Find and Find and Rename functions from the Edit menu help you locate specific files or text and perform replace operations on Mod names.


The Find function from the Edit menu helps you locate files or text. The scope of the search operation depends on which element in the UI has focus. This means that the Find operation works on the entire Profile when you click on a Mod name or works on the text when you click in the Mod Notes area.
